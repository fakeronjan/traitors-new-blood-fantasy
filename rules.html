The Draft

All 22 castmates get drafted before the season starts. Picks go in a fixed repeating order: Damon, then Em, then Ronjan, then back to Damon, and so on until all 22 are taken. Since 22 doesn't divide evenly by three, Damon picks first each cycle and ends up with 8 players; Em and Ronjan each get 7.

Once a player is drafted, every point they earn for the rest of the season goes to their owner. There's no trading and no free agency; whoever you draft is who you're stuck with.

Scoring

Points are logged episode by episode as events happen to your players. A player can rack up multiple events in one episode (surviving and picking up a murder credit, for example). Once a player is murdered or banished, their game is over and they stop earning points - no penalty for it, they just stop accumulating.

EventPoints

What the events mean

  • Survived the episode - your player is still in the game when the credits roll. The steady weekly points.
  • Team's daily mission succeeded - the group hits the mission target and grows the prize pot. Every one of your still-active players gets this.
  • Won an individual mission advantage - your player personally wins a shield, extra vote, information, or similar edge.
  • Was a Traitor when the Traitors murdered someone - every currently-active Traitor gets credit, not just whoever physically did it. It's a group decision either way.
  • Was a Faithful when a Traitor got banished - every currently-active Faithful gets credit when the house catches one. Worth more than a murder credit since Traitors are rare.
  • Recruited as a new Traitor - your player starts the season a Faithful but gets turned mid-season. Big value swing since it switches them onto the murder-credit track instead of the banish-credit track.
  • Reached the Final Roundtable - your player makes it to the last vote of the season, whatever happens next.
  • Ended up on the winning side at the end - your player is one of the Faithfuls who splits the pot, or one of the Traitors who won it. This one's a 20-point pool split evenly among everyone on the winning side, not a flat bonus - a solo winner takes the full 20, a 4-way split is worth 5 each. A rare solo win should outscore a crowded group split, same as the real prize money.

Ties and edge cases

If the season throws a twist the list above doesn't cover, we'll log the closest matching event and note it in the episode log so it's clear why the points landed the way they did.
